Hymn of the Sons

We followed Him thru wicked storms — horrid, bloody fierce,
We kept the pace ’til freedom’s Dawn sang true in ev’ry ear.
We trafficked jagged highways with daggers bared and bright,
We bore the weak thru vantablack of Serpent’s needled night.

We did indeed these talents turn to free the single flow’r,
whose diamond light they nectar’d-dim since that fateful hour.
We raked the seas for Willows the Fallen Sun would claim,
and struck back the dark, alas once more — with frigid, Holy Flame.

Now we commission galleons whose berth we’ll never meet;
They shall pirate ever on, those blistered, wretched seas.
Our abacus is quiet-kept, we’re absent honor’s roll;
we dive, we drown, we reif as one — we ransom for the Pearl.
